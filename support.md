# Weather Riot Support

**Weather with attitude. Support without it.**

Need help with Weather Riot or have a suggestion? Contact RIOT APPS at [riotapps@icloud.com](mailto:riotapps@icloud.com).

When reporting a problem, please include:

- Your iPhone or iPad model.
- Your iOS or iPadOS version.
- What happened and what you expected.
- A screenshot, if helpful.

Please avoid sending your precise location or other sensitive information.

## Weather isn’t loading

Check your internet connection, then refresh the weather in the app.

To use your current location, open your device’s **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Weather Riot** and allow location access while using the app.

You can also search for a city without granting device-location access.

If the app displays **DEMO WEATHER**, the values shown are sample data rather than live conditions.

## Widgets look blank or out of date

After installing or updating Weather Riot, open the app and let a live forecast load once. This sets the location used by your Home Screen widgets.

Widgets can then request updated weather independently while the app is closed. They request refreshes approximately every 30 minutes, but Apple controls the actual timing. Updates may take longer depending on device conditions and usage.

Widgets display weather for the last location loaded in the app. They do not track your movement in the background. To change the widget location, open Weather Riot and load weather for your current location or another city.

If a refresh fails, widgets may temporarily display a saved forecast with its update time. Check your internet connection and open Weather Riot to load fresh weather.

If a widget remains blank after live weather loads successfully, try removing it from your Home Screen and adding it again.

## Change your temperature units

Open **Settings** inside Weather Riot to change your temperature preference.

## Search for a city or save a favorite

Tap the search icon on the main screen to find a city. Select the city to view its weather, or tap its star to save it.

Tap the main screen’s star icon to open your favorite places.

## Weather information

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather. Forecasts can change. Do not rely on Weather Riot for emergency warnings or decisions involving personal safety.
