# Weather Riot Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 23, 2026**

Weather Riot is developed by Christopher Bradford under the RIOT APPS name. This policy explains how Weather Riot handles information when you use the app and its Home Screen widgets.

## Location and weather

With your permission, Weather Riot accesses your device’s location while you use the app to retrieve local weather through Apple WeatherKit and identify your city through Apple’s geocoding service. These requests send location information to Apple.

Weather Riot does not request background location access or maintain a history of your device’s movements. We do not operate a server that receives your location or weather requests.

You can use city search and saved places without granting access to your device’s location.

## City searches and favorite places

Searching for a city sends your search text to Apple Maps. Selecting a location sends its coordinates to Apple to retrieve weather and location information.

Favorite place names and coordinates are saved locally on your device. You can remove them from the app’s favorites list.

## Preferences and widgets

Weather Riot stores preferences, such as your temperature unit, on your device.

The app shares the most recently loaded city name, weather forecast, update time, and temperature unit with its Home Screen widgets through on-device shared storage.

The shared cache also includes the forecast location’s coordinates, rounded to two decimal places, so widgets can request updated weather directly from Apple WeatherKit while the app is closed. Widgets refresh the last loaded location; they do not track your movement or request background device location.

Apple controls widget refresh timing. If a refresh fails, widgets may show the previously loaded forecast with its update time. Open the app and select another place to change the widget location.

## Apple services

Weather Riot uses Apple services, including WeatherKit, Maps, and geocoding, to provide its features. Apple processes requests under its applicable terms and privacy policies.

For more information, see [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Accounts, advertising, and tracking

Weather Riot does not require a user account and does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising tracking.

Device diagnostics and any diagnostic information shared through Apple are governed by your device settings and Apple’s policies.

## Support communications

If you contact us at riotapps@icloud.com, we use your email address and the contents of your message to respond and help resolve your request.

Please avoid including sensitive information or your precise location in support messages.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request and satisfy applicable obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us.

## Your choices and data retention

You can deny or revoke location permission in your device’s Settings. Revoking permission stops future device-location requests; previously loaded weather may remain visible.

You can delete favorite places within the app and change your preferences in Settings.

Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to your device’s backup and restore behavior. Data you have emailed to us is handled separately as described above.

If live weather is unavailable, the app may display clearly labeled demonstration weather.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Weather Riot’s features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
